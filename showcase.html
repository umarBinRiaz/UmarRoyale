WHY UMAR ROYALE

Distilled by Hand in Small Batches

How long does a bottle last?

Our 38% extrait concentration lasts 8–12 hours on skin and can exceed a year with everyday wear.

Yes. We deliver nationwide via insured courier, with complimentary engraving on orders above Rs. 50,000.

Is the collection tested on animals?

Never. UMAR ROYALE is 100% cruelty-free and proudly certified vegan.

Absolutely. Book a private composition session with our master perfumer in Paris or remotely.
