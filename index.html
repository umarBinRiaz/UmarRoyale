05 Guidance

Frequently Asked

How long does a bottle last?

Our 38% extrait concentration lasts 8–12 hours on skin and, with everyday wear, a 50ml flacon can exceed one year.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We deliver worldwide via insured courier, with complimentary engraving on all orders above $500.

Is the collection tested on animals?

Never. UMAR ROYALE is 100% cruelty-free, proudly vegan, and our base ingredients are sustainably sourced.

Can I commission a bespoke fragrance?

Absolutely. Book a private composition session with our master perfumer — held in Paris or remotely by appointment.
